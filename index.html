Meet The Candidate

Driven by Integrity,
Tested by Public Service.

Born into a working-class family in Sapele, Melvin Akpomiemie Kogor learned early that real leadership isn't about lofty promises—it's about measurable results.Having done Business as a public servant and helping families from corporate overreach, he has decided to be elected into the Delta State House of Assembly.

Melvin Akpomiemie Kogoro vision are centred on URBAN RENEWAL, YOUTHS INCLUSION, ACCOUNTABLE REPRESENTATION FILLED WITH STRATEGIC LAWS TO CURB OFF POVERTY AND INSECURITY.

Accountable Governance

Sustainable Growth

Investing in jobs that pay modern living wages.

Platform & Vision

Our Four Pillars For Progress

Click through our core manifesto pillars to see detailed legislative roadmaps and commitments.

Economy

Fair Growth & Middle-Class Revival

Establish targeted tax relief for small businesses, penalize offshore corporate loopholes, and invest $200B into domestic advanced manufacturing and robotics hubs.

  • $20/hr Federal Minimum Wage Indexing
  • Small Business Innovation Tax Credits
  • Equal Pay Transparency Legislation
Target Timeline: Day 100
Healthcare

Universal Coverage & Lower Prescription Costs

Cap insulin and life-saving prescription drug costs at $35/month for all citizens, expand Medicare to cover dental and vision, and fund rural hospital modernizations.

  • Prescription Price Negotiation Power
  • Mental Healthcare Parity Enforcement
  • Rural Health Clinic Grants
Target Timeline: Year 1
Education

Debt-Free College & Teacher Pay Investment

Raise baseline public school teacher salaries to $65,000 nationwide, expand tuition-free community colleges, and cap federal student loan interest rates at 1%.

  • Universal Pre-K Funding
  • STEM & Vocational Apprenticeship Grants
  • Student Loan Interest Restructuring
Target Timeline: Year 1
Climate & Tech

100% Clean Grid & Ethical AI Standards

Accelerate the transition to a net-zero power grid by 2035 through nuclear & solar expansion, while establishing strict ethical guardrails for artificial intelligence.

  • Next-Gen Clean Energy Tax Rebates
  • AI Consumer Data Privacy Bill of Rights
  • Resilient Infrastructure Modernization
Target Timeline: Year 2
